# Community Code of Conduct

Maytrix welcomes respectful, constructive collaboration.

## Expected

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give useful feedback and accept corrections calmly.
- Respect privacy, ownership, and permission.
- Keep reports accurate and appropriate for a general audience.
- Help keep public gameplay fair and non-disruptive.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted humiliation
- Publishing private information or credentials
- Spam, impersonation, or knowingly false safety claims
- Features or instructions whose purpose is to harm or disrupt others

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community.
